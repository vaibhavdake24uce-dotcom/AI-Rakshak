Before any dataset enters the model

  1. Download a representative sample and preserve the original file.
  2. Record source URL, retrieval date, licence/terms and geographic/time coverage.
  3. Audit columns, missing values, duplicates, timestamp formats and coordinates.
  4. Keep unknown labels unknown. Do not infer “not flooded” from missing imagery.
  5. Separate train/test by event or time to avoid leakage.
